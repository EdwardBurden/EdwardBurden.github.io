I was getting frustrated that all my previous projects had awful art, but I didn't want to just buy Unity Asset Store models and drop them into my games. I wanted to learn how assets are actually made and imported into Unity.

I started with Blender Guru's course and made three assets, shown below. Not all of them look great, but I'm happy with my progress. I then used what I'd learned to make assets for prototyping my new game, *Freight*, and being able to visualise how it would look really helped. Having more than moving blocks on screen also gave me the motivation to keep working on it.

## Cupcake

<div class="row align-items-center mb-5">
  <img class="col-md-6 col-12" src="/assets/images/Blenders/Muffin.png" alt="Rendered cupcake">
  <p class="col-md-6 col-12">
    After finishing the donut tutorial, I set myself a homework task: build a cupcake using the techniques from a few different tutorials. I found it very challenging and I'm not 100% happy with the result. I struggled to get the shape of the base right, and I had problems texturing the paper wrapper too.
  </p>
</div>
